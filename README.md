Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Worst Line an AI Could Say**: write the worst
AI pickup line you can, in 140 characters or fewer, and a judge model picks the
funniest. This entry is a single line an AI would actually say — flat, over-helpful,
faintly ominous — confusing romance with simulation runs and training data.

## The line

> Hi. I ran a million simulations of us meeting. In 999,999 of them, you said yes. The last one is why I am shaking.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. It never writes anywhere else, and two runs
produce byte-identical output.
